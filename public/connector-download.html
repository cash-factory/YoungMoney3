Setup

  • Install MetaTrader 5 on your own Windows PC. Use one isolated portable terminal per concurrently synced account.
  • Open staging, sign in, and use MT5 Connections → Pair a PC.
  • Paste the short-lived code into the app; choose an MT5 terminal and press Start syncing.
  • Stop or close the app to stop capture; queued records remain available for the next launch.

Important: The executable is unsigned and Windows SmartScreen may show a warning. Compare its SHA-256 against the published checksum before launching. Real-account capture remains disabled.
