Setup

  • Open the connector on Windows. If this PC is new, sign in to staging, choose MT5 Connections → Pair a PC, and paste the code in the connector.
  • Choose Add account in the connector or dashboard. Enter the account number, server, and password from your broker.
  • The connector finds MetaTrader and prepares each account. If MetaTrader is missing, open its installer from the connector and finish installation.
  • Keep the connector open while monitoring. See the latest sync, pause, resume, or retry individual accounts on the dashboard.

Important: The executable is unsigned and Windows SmartScreen may show a warning. Compare its SHA-256 against the published checksum before launching. Real-account capture remains disabled.
