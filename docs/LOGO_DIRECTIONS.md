# Young-Money-Fx logo directions

**Selected: 01 — Signal Monogram.** The owner chose this direction on 18 November 2026. It is refined into the scalable [platform mark](../public/visuals/youngmoney-mark.svg), browser favicon and connector's native Canvas branding.

Compare the concepts on the staging site's [`/brand-options.html`](https://ym-fx-staging.ethical-hacker263.workers.dev/brand-options.html). That page can preview the marks on light or dark surfaces. The alternative directions remain archived; they are not additional selectable application brands.

The four PNG explorations were created with the built-in image generation tool. Prompts requested simple, professional, monochrome trading-journal marks, crisp geometric silhouettes, transparent backgrounds, no text, lighting, mockups or money symbols. The concept-specific instructions were:

- **Signal Monogram:** two interlocking angular YM strokes; negative space suggests a measured price swing; legible at 24 px.
- **Candle Frame:** two precise candlestick bars in an open rounded square; a frame edge suggests a price step.
- **Precision Point:** a four-part diamond aperture with a vertical candlestick in its open center; balanced negative space.
- **Market Flow:** three parallel sculpted ribbon strokes that suggest market movement and pages in an open journal.

The production SVG uses the selected direction's shape language with simplified paths for reliable small-size rendering. Generated source images are kept unchanged.
